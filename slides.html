The problem

Layout rules are everywhere — and problems hide in small places

Rules change every time

Page limits, Letter vs A4, minimum font, margins. Utah's thesis checklist wants 1¼-inch side margins; our default example uses 1 inch.

Checking today = looking

Scroll through every page, guess font sizes, measure with a ruler, tick a checklist by hand.

Eyes can't measure

9 pt vs 10 pt, 0.9 vs 1.0 inch — invisible on screen until the submission bounces.

Our problem sample, page 4: every highlighted line sits outside the 1-inch margin. Real screenshot.

What it shows

Small fonts, wrong paper, tight margins, extra pages found on the right pages; scanned pages honestly marked Needs Review.

What it doesn't show

The files were generated by us. On one real 32-page PDF, AI and manual mode agreed (4 Fails) — but the values weren't checked by hand. No student test yet — no claim of 100% accuracy on real documents, no time-saving claim.

AI mode does not measure better — same checker. It makes PrintProof easier: “20 mm”, “Letter size”, and it flags rules it can't check.

Built in

PDF never sent to the AI · text stripped from tool outputs · report must match tools · per-run limits · append-only audit log · key masked.

Still open

Numbers inside AI sentences aren't auto-checked · a misread rule is shown, not blocked · 5 attacks = evidence, not proof.

Limits and next steps

What doesn't work yet — and the next month

Limits today

  • Scanned pages: flagged, not measured
  • Text inside images not measured
  • Only 4 rule types (no line spacing, font family…)
  • Tested on generated files only
  • Local prototype, no deployment

Next month

  • Wk 1 — 30–50 real PDFs + student time test
  • Wk 2 — OCR for scans and images
  • Wk 3 — more rules; user confirms AI's reading
  • Wk 4 — hosted app with privacy notice and per-user limits
